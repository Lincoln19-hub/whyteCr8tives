How I work

We connect and plan the project

At Whyte Creatives, we approach every project with dedication, creativity, and attention to detail. Our commitment is to provide more than just images and videos — we strive to create meaningful visuals that you can treasure, share, and use with confidence. Together we agree the story, the location, the mood and the must-have frames. Let's bring your vision to life.

2
How I work

On the session day

I arrive early and read the light before you arrive. Direction is gentle — most people relax within ten minutes. Between the set-ups I work quietly for the unscripted moments: the laugh, the glance, the exhale. Those are the frames families keep.

3
How I work

Editing and delivery

Each selected frame is hand-edited for colour, tone and skin, then mastered into a private online gallery. You receive print-ready files, shareable links for family and clients, and guidance on prints or albums. Highlights land first; the full gallery follows.
